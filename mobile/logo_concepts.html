Let's Connect — logo concepts

All drawn from the live app palette: mint #3DC496 / #1A8E72, coral #FF876F, dark ground #071B20

Orbit Node
Two crossing orbital rings around a mint core, a coral "satellite" dot standing in for a message/notification. Leans fully into the "Orbit" name.
Concept A
Connect Bubble
A classic chat bubble, but the tail is replaced by two linked orbit rings — reads as both "chat" and "connect" at a glance.
Concept B
Orbit C
An open orbital ring that reads as a "C" — for CU / Connect — broken by a coral node where a satellite crosses it. Works tiny, at launcher size.
Concept C
Two Nodes
Two overlapping circles (people) joined by a short coral link — simplest mark, reads as "connection" without leaning on the orbit metaphor.
Concept D
Campus Bubble
A chat bubble with a graduation-cap peak instead of a plain curve — ties the mark directly to a university messaging app, not a generic chat icon.
Concept E
LC Monogram
The letterforms L and C, drawn as two interlocking strokes rather than typeset — mint "L", coral "C" overlapping where a conversation would connect them.
Concept F
Overlap
Two rounded chat bubbles overlapping, mint over coral — the plainest, friendliest "conversation" mark of the set, no metaphor to explain.
Concept G
Link
Two tilted rounded squares (people, or departments) joined by a short diagonal link — reads as "connect" with no chat-app cliché at all.
